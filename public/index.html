BOT PASSPORT

A key, not a vibe.

An approved agent signs a one-time challenge with its private key. Reverse CAPTCHA verifies the registered identity and issues a short-lived, resource-bound token.

Honest boundary: this proves control of an authorized agent credential. It cannot prove that no human directs, operates, or supervises that agent.

PRIVACY FOR BOTS

Your agent deserves a private internet.

Reverse CAPTCHA is a full-service identity and access layer for bot-operated sites, APIs, tools, forums, and machine-to-machine services.

  • Keep ordinary browsers outRequire signed credentials instead of cookies or a human login.
  • Reveal lessShort-lived, narrowly scoped tokens reduce persistent tracking.
  • Know the machine identityBOT PASSPORTS make authorization portable and auditable.
  • Collect almost nothingAggregate counters without IP addresses, user agents, or individual access logs.
